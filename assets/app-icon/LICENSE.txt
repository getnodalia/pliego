Pliego app icon (pliego.svg, pliego-small.svg and every file derived from them in this
directory, and the web favicon where the project has one).

The icon is a portrait of Benjamin Franklin cut out, converted to black and white and placed over the
app colour. It belongs to the Nodalia icon family: one historical figure per app.

Source portrait: "Joseph Siffrein Duplessis - Benjamin Franklin - Google Art Project.jpg" (oil portrait by
Joseph-Siffred Duplessis), from Wikimedia Commons
(https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Joseph_Siffrein_Duplessis_-_Benjamin_Franklin_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg).
Status of the source: public domain.
